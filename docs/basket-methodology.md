# Basket Methodology

The basket is a user-defined group of essential goods and services.

## Example Basket

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Mealie meal | 25kg |
| Cooking oil | 2L |
| Sugar | 2kg |
| Rice | 5kg |
| Bread | 4 loaves |
| Eggs | 1 tray |
| Charcoal | 1 bag |
| Data bundle | 1 monthly bundle |

## Basket Cost

For each item:

basket_item_cost = normalized_price_per_unit × basket_quantity

Total basket cost:

total_basket_cost = sum(basket_item_cost)

## Monthly Basket Inflation

monthly_change = ((current_month_cost - previous_month_cost) / previous_month_cost) × 100

## Important Notes

The basket is personal and should not be treated as official national inflation.
